## Plain English: what we decided, why, and what the labels mean

**Audience:** the human operator. This section is the SSoT (source of truth) for
plain language about announce-bar B, the "held" list, and the claim pins. Agent
shorthand lives below; **this section wins for understanding.**

### What you asked for (and what went wrong)

You asked for real reasoning in ordinary American English, not a status table
that assumes you already know residual codes. The implement report for the
announce residual closed the paperwork under **Option B** but failed to unpack
*why* B was the honest choice, what "held non-default" means, and what each pin
is actually saying. That was a process failure: **status without explanation is
not a plan answer.** This section fixes that on disk.

### Two different questions people mix up

| Question | Plain meaning |
|----------|---------------|
| **May we publicly describe the product as full-readiness *as this program defined it*?** | "Is the scoreboard of in-scope work green with evidence, so an announce is honest about what we finished?" |
| **May we claim "we have a finished production LLVM backend"?** | "Is the living product flag `fullBackend` (also named `llvmCfgFixtureFullBackendClaimed` in host Lean) set to true because every bar item that *that* claim requires is proven?" |

Those are **not** the same question. Option B is about the **first** question.
Option A is about the **second**. Treating them as one question is how you get
either a silent lie (flip the flag early) or a permanent stall (refuse to
announce anything until a different, heavier claim is also true).

### What Option A and Option B were

When progressive Full LLVM, foreign link, and Rust IR work were done, the
remaining "announce residual" asked:

- **Option A:** Keep the old rule "you may not announce full readiness until the
  production full-backend claim pin is true." Then go do whatever is still
  missing for that pin, prove it with the host Lean elaborator (today that means
  a real `lake build` of the claim surface), and only then announce.
- **Option B:** Change the announce rule so full readiness announce may proceed
  when the **progressive full-readiness scoreboard** is green, **without**
  requiring the production full-backend claim pin to be true. Keep that pin
  **false**. Say out loud in public framing that the production backend claim
  is still not asserted.

**Chosen: B.** Written 2026-08-10 in residual, this plan, bar research, and handoff.

### Why B (actual reasoning, not a slogan)

1. **You already ordered "do the remaining progressive work," not "stall forever
   behind one pin."** Earlier exclusive A/B/C menus were rejected. Progressive
   Full LLVM (path B), Mult through Graph foreign consumers, Rust IR partial and
   multi-band full correspondence, package check depth under the named measured
   bar, dual present-partial honesty, and residual join lockstep are **done**
   with gates. The open item was only *how announce rules relate* to the
   production full-backend flag.

2. **At announce-close under bar B, the production full-backend tip stayed false
   on purpose.** Progressive work was green; that did **not** authorize renaming
   progressive evidence into the stronger tip claim. The written bar still
   separated (and still separates for non-tip claims):
   - progressive evidence we *have* (control-flow graphs dual-pinned to on-disk
     LLVM text, partial dominance-frontier tables, multi-node IR walks, foreign
     consumer contracts, Rust layout correspondence), from
   - heavier claims we *do not* make from progressive alone (full dominance
     pipeline claimed, machine-code optimization pipeline claimed, band-local
     Mult..Graph FullBackend flags).
   Flipping the tip **only** because progressive work was green would have been
   forgery ("do not forge fullBackend"). **Later history:** Package 1 A-light
   closed a separate residual that flipped fixture tip `fullBackend` **true**
   with lake + join redesign (band FullBackend / production opt / DominanceClaimed
   still false).

3. **Rust IR multi-band success is not a synonym for fullBackend.** We built a
   real multi-band joint dual map (our Mult, Linear, Types, Program, Graph
   contracts agreeing with layout numbers and an out-of-tree Rust dogfood crate).
   That is intercompatibility evidence for **those** contracts. It is **not**
   "we formalized all of rustc," **not** product Rust living under `src/`, and
   **not** permission to set the production full-backend pin true.

4. **Path B already taught the same honesty pattern.** For dominance-frontier
   work we decided a *partial* DF table plus general CFG posture is enough for
   progressive bar item (2), while a *full* dominance pipeline claim stays
   false. Announce-bar B is the same pattern at the announce layer: progressive
   readiness can be true for announce while a heavier production claim stays
   false and named.

5. **Option A was future work at announce-close; Package 1 later closed it.**
   At the moment bar B closed announce, tip fullBackend was still false and
   Option A (claim tip true with lake under the honesty bar) was **not** required
   for announce. Agents were not to auto-queue A without a checkable Open residual.
   **Later history:** Package 1 A-light **did** open and close that residual:
   fixture tip true with lake elaborator proof + honesty bar SSoT still green.
   Option A is **not** a living Open residual now. Remaining non-claims are
   production opt, DominanceClaimed / full DF pipeline, band-local FullBackend,
   mathlib / FullHost day-one -- not tip fullBackend.

6. **What announce under B is allowed to say (and not say).**
   - **May say:** freestanding product free and self-host complete; PROVABLY with
     CompCert; host day-to-day tools without Lake on the step under free bar
     choice (a); progressive LLVM CFG/SSA evidence; Mult through Graph foreign
     consumers; Rust IR correspondence partial and multi-band full under our
     bar; fixture tip fullBackend **true** under Package 1 A-light; runtime
     benches; Formal Vibefication origin.
   - **Must still say is not claimed:** production opt; full dominance pipeline
     (DominanceClaimed); machine-code optimization pipeline; band-local
     Mult..Graph FullBackend flags; full Lean 4 / mathlib-scale elaborator;
     FullHost package elaborate as a finished "everything Lake does forever"
     story; product Rust under `src/`; formalized entire rustc; PROVABLY means
     "fast." (Tip fullBackend A-light **is** claimed true; do not re-list it
